## Poetry
Poetry can be soothing, emotional, aggressive or even heart-felt depending on what type of poetry you like. For me, I like any type of poetry. Written, spoken and even potery slams. But, I do written poetry. You don't have to think too hard about what to write as long as you have a topic you are geniunely interested in or maybe you need to get something off your mind and chest. It makes me feel less pressure and more uplifted because I have a book to communicate with about my thoughts, emotions and opinions. Poetry slams give me goosebumps which is a good thing. I feel connected just like how I feel with written and spoken poetry. It speaks to the audience and a lot of meanings that we may or may not know. Most of all, it is a little to no judgment zone in what you write, say or do. Poetry slams come with feelings from the soul and body language in which can relate to the targeted audience.
